Cakes

Cake is a flour confection made from flour, sugar, and other ingredientts and is usually baked. In their oldest forms, cakes were modifications fo bread, but cakes now cover a wide range of preparations that can be simple or elaborate and which share features with desserts such as pastries, meringues, custards, and pies.

Cakes is often as a celebratory dish on ceremonial occasions, such as weddings, annivasaries, and birthdays. There are countless cake recipes; some are bread-like, some are rich and elaborate, and many are centuries old. Cake making is no longer a complicated procedure; while at one time considerable labor went into making (particularly the whisking of egg foams), baking equipment and directions have been simplified so that even the most amateur of cooks may bake a cake.
